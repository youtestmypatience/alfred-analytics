# Литература и документация

## Основные книги

1. Dan Linstedt, Michael Olschimke — *Building a Scalable Data Warehouse with Data Vault 2.0*. [Elsevier / ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/9780128025109/building-a-scalable-data-warehouse-with-data-vault-20)
2. Ralph Kimball, Margy Ross — *The Data Warehouse Toolkit*, 3rd edition. [Kimball Group](https://www.kimballgroup.com/data-warehouse-business-intelligence-resources/books/data-warehouse-dw-toolkit/)
3. Joe Reis, Matt Housley — *Fundamentals of Data Engineering*. [O’Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/fundamentals-of-data/9781098108298/)
4. W. H. Inmon — *Building the Data Warehouse*. [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Building+the+Data+Warehouse%2C+4th+Edition-p-9780764599446)
5. Michael J. Hernandez — *Database Design for Mere Mortals*.
6. C. J. Date, Hugh Darwen, Nikos Lorentzos — *Temporal Data & the Relational Model*.
7. Richard T. Snodgrass — *Developing Time-Oriented Database Applications in SQL*.

Полные тексты коммерческих книг в проект не включены. Используются легальные страницы издателей и официальная документация.
